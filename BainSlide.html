3 Barriers to Imitability
Barrier 01
Interior-Specific Installer Network (ADG)
ADG's 3,200+ specialized interior installers took decades to recruit and certify. Amazon cannot replicate trade labor at scale; HD's SRS is exterior-focused (roofing, landscaping) — creating an uncrowded interior finish vertical.
Barrier 02
Drywall Distribution Network Density (FBM)
FBM's 370+ branches serve ~40,000 Pro accounts with next-day jobsite delivery of heavy, low-margin drywall/framing products. The economics favor incumbent density — building a competing branch network requires $2–3B+ capex and 5–7 years of relationship-building.
Barrier 03
Combined Data Moat: Retail + Distribution + Install
Lowe's uniquely captures the full project lifecycle — design selection, materials purchase, distribution, and installation — creating a purchasing data loop no pure retailer (HD is exterior-heavy) or pure distributor (Ferguson is trade-only) can replicate across the interior finish category.
